# Recap · Matchnight (11 Nov 2026)

The owner's redesign of the Recap (home), approved on the design canvas "Sofix Recap: Matchnight"
(https://claude.ai/artifact/B1xXjN5n7wXARGu1EXof7T, private) after four rounds of comments. "build the Recap".

## Requested outcome

- **Hero:** the week's ten best cards fanned like a hand (best in the middle, hover lifts one and names him under the fan),
  each with its xS hexagon, start %, and once played a stamp with what he scored (DNP when he did not play). A huge outlined
  GW number behind. The one big number is what you won (largest essence first), or the Sofix plan's expected essence
  before anything is paid. Status pill (Final / locks …), Open the plan.
- **You vs Sofix:** one compact grid under the hero, rows You (N Lineups) and Sofix plan (N Lineups), columns Rewards % and
  each reward kind; the better value of each column highlighted; a verdict line when both are known. Replaces the Rewards card.
- **Missions** right after the hero.
- **Your lineups:** Entered / Sofix plan switch. Each lineup: Sorare's competition icon on its colour, PRO pill for Pro,
  in-season / Classic mark (no card count); the cards as an overlapping hand with Sorare's score under each (total with
  bonuses below it); a score track (actual against an xS marker, Pro's target line); xR (Play's projected prize) and Won.
- **The round:** one competition dropdown (All competitions + only those with games this week) and one day dropdown
  (All days + only days with games). Each competition is a row of ticket stubs that always fills the width; three games or
  fewer become wide tickets. Stubs carry xG and xD chips in score-band colours, the win/draw/win bar, the score, "Upset"
  when the favourite lost, "No forecast" when there is none. A match sheet for the picked game with banded butterfly bars.
- **Table:** a skyline of 20 bars (crest in a disc, points), UEFA colours from the official logos (Champions League
  #00004b 1-4, Europa League #ff6900 5, Conference League #01b223 6, relegation red 18-20), places moved in the round,
  next game's FDR under each; switch After round N / Now.
- **News this week** unchanged. Team news unchanged.
- **App-wide:** Sorare's pearl for a perfect 100 (gradient #eae3da, #c0e6ef, #d8e4f4, #e4cbe7, #e8f6ef with a glow) wherever a
  score band colour is drawn; "xS", never "xScore", in UI copy.
- Phone 390 px: hand smaller, stubs and skyline scroll sideways, lineups stack. National-team week keeps AwayWeek.
