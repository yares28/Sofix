Why two clicks

Sorare has a draft. createOrUpdateSo5Lineup takes draft: true, which saves a lineup without entering it, and confirmSo5Lineups enters it afterwards. So the first click is reversible and the second is the one that spends a card slot or an entry fee.

Sorare checks it first. previewSo5Lineup returns a state per rule, the bonuses it will really apply and the reward multiplier — so the sheet shows Sorare's own verdict, not ours, before anything is written. When the two disagree, that is a bug in Sofix and it is shown, not hidden.

Only your browser can do it. The API key is read-only; entering a lineup needs the sorare.com session, which lives in the extension. Apply is the one thing here that cannot happen while your PC is off.
